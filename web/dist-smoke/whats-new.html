What's new

What you can do with DMbot now, newest first. Only things that are live are here.

  1. Your questions to the team

    16 October 2026

    After you write to the DMbot team, your account page lists your questions, with the date you sent each one and whether it is waiting, answered or closed.

  2. Quick answers use your facts

    16 October 2026

    When you ask DMbot a quick question about someone in your campaign, it now uses the facts you confirmed, like who they work for. If it has nothing confirmed, it says so.

  3. Sign in with an emailed link

    15 October 2026

    On your account page, type your email and we send you a link that signs you in. It works once and stops working after 15 minutes.

  4. Your DMbot ID

    15 October 2026

    Your account page now shows your DMbot ID, which is your account number and not a password, and your experience points, which count the minutes DMbot has run for you.

  5. Share house rules with other tables

    15 October 2026

    A game master on a paid plan can share their house rules with a share code. Another table can use them, keep its own changes, and get updates when the rules change, and the kinds of link between people, places and things that your table made can travel with a set if you tick them.

  6. Suggest a rule back

    15 October 2026

    A table that uses shared rules can send one of its own rules to the game master who shares them. It goes with no name, and nothing changes until that game master adds it.

  7. One-of-a-kind items

    15 October 2026

    On an item's name card, a game master can press Only one exists, so DMbot knows there is only one. Press Allow more than one to undo it.

  8. Ask DMbot by typing

    15 October 2026

    Anyone who said yes can type a quick rules question in the table's dmb-chat channel. The short answer is posted there for everyone, with its source, and the game master has the final say.

  9. The game clock

    13 October 2026

    The game master keeps the story's day and time in DMbot. Players see it in their own channel, and the game master sees the exact time.

Need a hand? Read the Help page.
