Terms of use

The short version

DMbot listens to your game in Discord and quietly helps the game master (DM). It only records people who said yes, and everyone in the server can read what it writes down. DMbot gives advice; the game master decides. It can mishear and make mistakes, so check anything important. Plans are paid monthly through our payment company, and you can change or stop any time. Don't use DMbot to record people who haven't agreed, and only share books or adventures you have the right to use.

Who we are

DMbot is run by DMbot LLC ("we"). Write to dmbot.admin@gmail.com. By adding DMbot to a server or signing in, you agree to these terms.

Who can use DMbot

  • You must be at least 16, and follow Discord's own rules.
  • The person who adds DMbot to a server, or runs a campaign, is responsible for using it fairly in that server.

Recording and saying yes

  • DMbot records only people who said yes (the "I consent" button or/consent give). Anyone can stop at any time: press ⚙️ Menu in DMbot's private message to you, then "Stop recording me", then "Yes". Or type/consent revoke.
  • What DMbot writes down can be read by everyone in that Discord server, and downloaded on paid plans.
  • Don't try to record people who haven't agreed, for example by playing their voice through someone else's microphone.

What DMbot does, and doesn't

  • DMbot gives the game master quiet advice. It never makes up story and never decides. The game master decides.
  • DMbot can mishear words, mix up names, and get rules wrong. Every rules tip shows where it comes from and how sure DMbot is. Check anything that matters.
  • We work hard to keep DMbot running, but we can't promise it never stops or never loses a line. DMbot is provided as is, with no promise it will always be available.

Plans and payment

  • Plans, prices and hours are on the Prices page. Paid plans renew every month until you stop them. Try It never turns into a paid plan by itself, and it needs no payment details. To keep going after Try It, pick a plan.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe, who is the seller. Their terms apply to the payment, and they add any sales tax.
  • You can change or stop your plan any time from My Account. A stopped plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • If a renewal payment fails, there is a short grace period to fix it before the plan stops. Tap Fix my payment in My Account. If your first payment doesn't go through, the plan doesn't start until it does. If the plan stops, campaigns are kept for 120 days, then deleted.
  • Refunds are explained on the Refunds page.
  • Prices may change at any time. The price shown when you buy is the price you pay for that period.

Your campaign and your words

  • Your campaign, notes and what your table says belong to you and your table, not to us. You let us store and process them only to run DMbot for you. Within the table, the campaign is the game master's: they run it, and only they can delete it.
  • When a game master shares an adventure or a rulebook, they confirm they have the right to use it. We don't check this and give no legal advice. It stays in that one campaign, and the game master can delete it.
  • What DMbot makes from a session belongs to the campaign. That means a recap, story facts, notes for the game master, and anything else DMbot writes from what was said. The campaign belongs to its game master.
  • If you stop being recorded, take back your yes or delete your account, DMbot stops recording you and removes what the Privacy page says it removes. What it already made from sessions you took part in stays with the campaign. Only deleting the campaign removes it, and only the campaign's game master can delete a campaign.
  • If someone tells us shared material breaks their rights, we may remove it. Write to dmbot.admin@gmail.com with a link to the material.
  • Backups of a paid campaign hold the campaign, including the game master's secrets. Anyone in the server may download one. Whoever loads a backup becomes the game master of that copy.

Fair use

Don't use DMbot to break the law, to harass anyone, to share content Discord doesn't allow, or to get around plan limits. Don't try to break into DMbot or other people's campaigns. We may pause or close an account that does this.

Ending

  • You can delete your account any time from My Account. Deleting removes your sign-in details and ends your plan at once; you won't be charged again, and the rest of the month isn't paid back. If you want money back, ask before you delete (see Refunds). Your campaigns are not deleted with your account, because the other players may still want to read or download them. They are deleted 120 days later, unless another DM of the campaign takes them over. If the owner's Discord account is gone, or the owner has been out of the server for 7 days, DMbot offers the campaign's other game masters a button to take the campaign on. Only a game master who presses it takes over.
  • We may stop offering DMbot. If we do, we'll give paid users at least 30 days' notice and time to download their backups.
