Refunds

The short version

You can stop your plan any time. It keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for.

If we charged you by mistake, or twice, we give the money back.

Extra hours can't be refunded. For anything else, ask us and we'll look at it fairly.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with.

Stopping your plan

Stop your plan any time from My Account. You won't be charged again. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for. After that, your campaigns are kept for 120 days in case you come back.

Deleting your account is not the same as stopping your plan: DMbot stops for you at once, you won't be charged again, and the rest of the month you paid for isn't paid back. If you want money back, write to us before you delete your account (see How to ask).

When you can get money back

  • A plan you just started and haven't used: write to dmbot.admin@gmail.com within 14 days of paying and we'll refund it. A monthly renewal is not a new plan.
  • Charged by mistake, or charged twice: we refund it.
  • DMbot didn't work for a long time because of a problem on our side: ask us, and we'll look at each case. Write to dmbot.admin@gmail.com.
  • Extra hours (the add-on hours you buy) can't be refunded, used or not.

Stripe is the seller. If you are in the EU or the UK, it handles your legal right to withdraw, and your invoices and tax.

How to ask

Write to dmbot.admin@gmail.com from the email on your account, and say what happened. We answer within 5 working days. Refunds are made by Stripe to the card or account you paid with, and can take a few days to show.

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Questions? Write to dmbot.admin@gmail.com. You can also delete your account and data fromMy Account.
