Questions and answers

What counts as an hour?

The time DMbot is listening, from when you start it to when you stop it. Time when DMbot isn't listening doesn't count.

Do unused hours carry over?

No. Your hours are for the month. Next month you get a full set again. If you run out, you can add 10 more hours (on paid plans).

What's different between the plans?

Every plan has the same DMbot. Plans differ only in hours and campaigns (the free Try It has no backups or downloads): it writes down what’s said at the table, remembers the names your table makes up, helps with rules, remembers your story, and keeps backups. Narrated recaps with music are an extra: Guild and Pro include them, and you can add them to the other paid plans. Each campaign gets up to 10 recaps a month. Up to 8 of those can be narrated, with a voice and music.

Can I change my plan?

Yes, any time, from My Account.

What if I move to a plan with fewer campaigns?

Nothing is deleted. The first campaigns you play after the change keep going, up to your new limit. The rest pause. To play a paused one, move to a bigger plan.

What if a payment doesn't go through?

If a renewal payment fails, your plan keeps working for a short grace period while you fix it. Tap Fix my payment in My Account. If your first payment doesn't go through, the plan doesn't start until it does.

How long do you keep my campaign?

After your last game: 60 days on Try It, 6 months on Table, and 1 year on the other plans. If you stop paying, we keep it for 120 days. We message you on Discord 14 days and 3 days before anything is deleted.

Can I give a campaign to someone else?

Yes. In Discord, open ⚙️ Settings and tap 🤝 Hand over, then pick who should take it. They have 7 days to say yes. Nothing changes until they do; then it uses their hours, not yours.

Can I delete everything?

Yes, from My Account. Your account and the campaigns you run go straight away. Backups people downloaded, and lines you said in other people's games, stay.
