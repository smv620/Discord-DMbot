House Aumery holds the eastern march from its seat at Greystone, a castle of pale stone above the Ferrow river. The house colours are green and gold, and its banner flies over the gatehouse on every feast day. Each midwinter the lords of the march gather in the great hall for the tourney, and the winning knight receives a silver horn.

The present lord is Anselm Aumery, a tall and careful man who is respected at court. He speaks well at council, pays his garrison on time, and has never lost a border dispute. His hall is known for its generosity, and travellers are fed there without charge for three nights.

Lord Anselm has an heir, Edric, who is the son of his late wife. Edric is not in fact Anselm's blood. The true heir, Cressida, was the child of Anselm's elder brother, and she was sent north as a babe to be raised by a miller's family under another name. Anselm adopted Edric to keep the title from her.

Under the chapel of Greystone runs a passage that leads to the old postern, which has been bricked up for a century. The passage was dug by Anselm's grandfather, and only the castellan and the lord know where the iron key is hidden behind the altar.

The castle's kitchens are busy this season. The cooks are baking for the tourney. The stables are full of horses from the coast. The garrison drills in the yard each morning before the bell.
