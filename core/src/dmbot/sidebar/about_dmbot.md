DMbot, in short (a reviewed digest of its help; the sidebar answers questions about DMbot from this and nothing else).

What it is: a Discord bot that listens to a D&D table's voice channel and privately helps the Dungeon Master (DM). The bot advises; the DM decides. It never makes up the story and never decides anything.

Recording: only people who say yes are recorded. When a session starts, or someone joins the voice channel, DMbot sends them a private message with an "I consent" button. Anyone can stop with the "Menu" button in that message, then "Stop recording me". Bots are never recorded.

Commands (the DM starts and stops; others can use some):
- /dmbot start: pick a campaign and voice channel, then start listening. /dmbot stop: stop listening.
- /dmbot names: the names DMbot listens for (characters, places, NPCs); the DM adds, edits, imports lists.
- /dmbot houserules: this campaign's house rules; only its DM adds, edits or removes them.
- /dmbot rule: look up a spell, condition or creature in the free rules (SRD); the DM only. Also under Settings, "Look up a rule".
- /dmbot optionalrules: turn optional rules from Xanathar's and Tasha's on or off.
- /transcript: download what was said in a session. /dmbot backup: download a complete copy of a campaign. /dmbot restore: bring a campaign back from a copy.

Transcripts: anyone in the server can read and download a campaign's transcripts (raw or cleaned). The DM screen's notes are not in them.

DM screen: a private channel for the DM with rules tips and notes. It is shown to players only if the DM allows it (only the DM, players can peek, or everyone).

Rules order: house rules first, then homebrew, then the campaign's chosen rules (2024 by default), then the older 2014 rules only when nothing newer exists (marked Legacy 2014). Only the free rules (SRD) are built in, not other books.

Plans: each DM's plan sets hours a month and how many campaigns they can run. Try It has no backups or downloads.
