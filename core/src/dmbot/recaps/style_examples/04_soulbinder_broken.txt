Boone raises Stonecleaver high over his head and looks to the heavens. The ground rumbles beneath him and the nightveils struggle to keep their footing as four red stars appear in the dark sky directly above them. The dots of twinkling fire spread apart suddenly and grow quickly, too quickly. In the instant it took the nightveils to realize that the four stars were meteors crashing towards them, it was too late. Boone had positioned the impact points so that the strikes formed craters around him, the edges of their fireballs licking within inches of himself. The nightveils that surrounded him, however, caught the full force of the meteor blast.

When the smoke cleared, Boone was covered in hot ash and there was not a trace left of the nightveils.

Meanwhile…

The heroes found their footing when Lightrender channels Virtue with Heavenly Words, causing their foes to suffer the effects of Power Word Stun. Seraveth had not had to deal with much from the heroes to this point, but she had been weakened just enough that she was not strong enough to resist the Heavenly Words. From there the heroes did not relent. They unleashed their full wrath upon Seraveth. Rook let his ki flow freely to impart a barrage of attacks, Tavish loosed his divine magic and empowered his attacks with holy smites, and Kaito unleashed spells that the stunned Seraveth could not avoid; with the stun, her concentration was broken. Boone's banishment was over, and he reappeared in the fray to help finish off the Soulbinder in a flurry of attacks.

As Seraveth fell, the winged sentinels surged to protect her, but with the Soulbinder's hold over them broken, they became free of the false thoughts that had consumed them. They took just a moment to look upon their former master, then turned away only long enough to thank the heroes for their freedom. Without waiting for a reply, they surged into the skies to join their kin and aid the allied forces at the front gate.

The hold over the tower was broken too, and Kaito was able to claim the great Sorcerer's Sanctum as his own. He now commanded the tower.
