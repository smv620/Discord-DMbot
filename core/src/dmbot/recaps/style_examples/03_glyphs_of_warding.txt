When we last left our heroes, they returned to the docks of Valdren to find a strange and surreal sight: the city seemed abandoned except for the festivities in the center square, where the Candlelight celebrations continued.

Our heroes and the Patriarch of the Light agreed that the heroes would warn the royal family while the Patriarch traveled north to the Crimson Abbey.

Our heroes met up again with Ysolde, the horned jewelcrafter who was boarding herself up in her shop. She was a bit confused when Kaito claimed he had never been to the Silver Spire or the isle of Vashti, the hubs of her people's civilization on this world. Ysolde had seen the attacks of the undead but seemed herself to have escaped the plague. She joined them on their way to the castle.

Our heroes were disturbed to see the castle had been besieged. Doors and gates were broken and splintered, with no sign of the guards or the royal family. Eventually making their way to the royal quarters, they found Prince Aldous's room in shambles. It had been searched, and evidently someone had been dragged to the window. Bodies were discovered in the courtyard below. One was the head magistrate of the courts. He carried a sealed letter, which Kaito took.

Torchlight in the gardens beyond caught their attention, and Kaito blasted his way through the labyrinth of hedges to find Prince Aldous himself trying to leave the castle grounds unseen.

Though the prince believed Kaito, he warned about bringing accusations against nobility without proof. The prince asked the heroes to search Lady Corvane's quarters for evidence before leveling such serious charges.

He told Kaito to find the evidence and use it to win support from the Crimson Abbey, then to meet him and his father at Dawnwatch Chapel in two days. The prince then summoned a spectral griffin to carry him away into the night sky as it began to rain.

The search of Lady Corvane's room revealed only a trap that had been laid with Pip as the bait. Pip reported that he had fled from Millbrook when Lionel Varrow started to purge the city. Pip had been beaten within an inch of his life, restrained to a chair in the middle of the room and surrounded by glyphs of warding. He reported that a few of the magistrate's guards had tortured him for information about Kaito and his friends, asking again and again for the whereabouts of "the artifact". A tall, hooded figure had placed runes of warding and destructive magic traps. The moment someone tried to leave the circle of glyphs, a devastation of ice and fire exploded, destroying the entire royal wing of the castle. Kaito had teleported out, leaving Pip and Tavish at ground zero. Boone's quick thinking protected the pair with a force field from his bead of force; however, they were now buried under thousands of tons of stone.

Kaito, Boone and Ysolde fled from the falling keep. Kaito told Boone and Ysolde that the prince had instructed him to head to the Crimson Abbey and gather political reinforcements.

The collapse of the castle is loud enough to be heard throughout the entire capital. Mobs of hundreds can now be seen in the distance, marching up the long hill of the main road towards the castle. The heroes do not have much time before an army of undead is upon them.
