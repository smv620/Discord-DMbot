Previously with the Heroes of Valdren: the Huntsman, a demon of the abyss, seemingly had a bounty on Kaito's head. The battle raged on as the three heroes frantically cut down hell hounds, hid behind walls of fire and light, and closed abyssal rifts as quickly as they could. Soldiers on the walls of Harrowgate fired ballistae and catapults at the evil creatures, but storms of necrotic arrows laid waste to the ill-equipped brave men and women of the guard.

Kaito was down but out of the Huntsman's reach. His body lay defenseless on top of the city wall. Fixated on the task, it seemed Kaito's death was not the goal; the Huntsman's forces wanted his corpse as a prize, or more likely, his soul.

Boone and Merel did everything in their power to stop the demon, and the delay induced by the height of the wall was just enough. They cut down the demon before he could fell a tree to gain access to Kaito's body.

A brave medic atop the wall revived Kaito, only to be struck down again by the icy breath of the hellhounds.

Eventually, the demon was defeated, but the heroes had expended every attack they could and needed rest. The fading words of the Huntsman haunt their victory: "Just a delay. I shall return for you, Son of Hallam. There will be no escape!"

A city guard captain scolds the heroes: "Whose damn wolf is this, scaring the hell out of the good people in the streets below?! It took us forever to get reinforcements up to the ramparts!"

Boone's wolf seemed apathetic to the critique as he sat and scratched his ear with his back left paw, followed by a long yawn.

Boone casually responded, "Oh. Ya. He's mine."

Osric, steward of Harrowgate, arrived shortly afterwards and met the heroes, who were still helping attend to the wounded and fallen on top of the wall. Merel resurrects the young medic who saved Kaito earlier, to the cheers of the crowd. Her heart is heavy with the many lost lives she could not save.

Osric escorts the heroes to his own home in the north of Harrowgate to give them food, rest and recovery. It is now late in the afternoon, and the heroes don't know how long they have before the Huntsman or his spies return. Food and beds are provided.

The next morning, the heroes rise early. Osric asks them to help find the baron's son in the Greyspine Mountains, but they don't want to draw the demonic forces into the city. They must complete their business here quickly. They don't know how long the Huntsman has been banished.
