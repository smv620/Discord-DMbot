Previously with the Heroes of Valdren:

Suddenly, as if compelled by a silent command, the grotesque arachnid army comes to an abrupt halt. The Frost Tyrant himself, a dark and looming specter, strides forth at the forefront, his baleful gaze scouring the horizon in the direction of the hidden allied host. Nightfrost strides along the air with hooves crackling on an invisible frozen path, his presence casting a pall of dread that darkens the shadows, an unnatural aura sapping the life from everything it touches. Behind the Frost Tyrant the spider-folk shift and sway in eerie unison, their glimmering forms creating a macabre dance within the inky sea of darkness. The unsettling clicking of their bone-like limbs reverberates through the cold night air; their chaotic rhythm forms a terrifying hum.

Fear radiates from the soldiers huddled in hiding, and their unheard prayers rush through their minds. Their trembling breaths form wispy plumes of fog in the frigid night. While those closest to Tavish find strength in the paladin's presence, the majority remain too distant to benefit from his reassuring aura. They shiver uncontrollably, the enormity of the impending battle becoming agonizingly clear. It is a living nightmare, a harrowing dance with death in the heart of a forsaken tundra.
