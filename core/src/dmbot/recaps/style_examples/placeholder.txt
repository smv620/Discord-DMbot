The rain had not stopped for two days, and the road to the border town had turned to mud. Nobody in the party spoke much. They walked with their hoods up and their eyes on the tree line, and when the lantern at the gate finally came into view, the relief was quiet and tired.

Inside, the keeper of the inn looked them over and set out bowls of broth without being asked. It was the first warm thing anyone had eaten in days. By the time the bowls were empty, the mood had changed: shoulders dropped, a laugh broke out, and a plan began to take shape over the scraps of bread.
