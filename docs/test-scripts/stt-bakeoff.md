# Bake-off script: campaign names (#128)

For the speech-to-text bake-off ([../STT_BAKEOFF.md](../STT_BAKEOFF.md)). **Each reader reads
every line**, recorded on their own device (not through DMbot).

**How to read**
- Read naturally, like at the table. Don't over-pronounce the names.
- **Pause about 2 seconds after every line** (count "one thousand, two thousand"). The tool
  cuts the recording at these pauses.
- Stumbled? Pause, then say the whole line again. Don't skip lines.
- Before you start, practise the names below once with the pronunciation guide, so both
  readers say them the same way. It takes about 6–8 minutes.

## The lines

1. Okay everyone, let's get started.
2. Last time, you had just arrived in Brynwater after three days on the road.
3. Cerric, you're at the front of the group. What do you do?
4. Cerric draws his sword and looks for tracks.
5. Belleros will take the lead while the others wait by the gate.
6. Hrothgar the blacksmith waves at you from across the square.
7. I want to talk to Hrothgar about the broken shield.
8. A tall elf named Mirelle steps out of the shadows.
9. Mirelle says the road to Dravenmoor is closed.
10. Can I make an Insight check on Mirelle?
11. Roll a d20 and add your Wisdom.
12. I rolled a natural twenty, plus three, so twenty-three.
13. You can tell she's hiding something about Vhalzimar.
14. Who is Vhalzimar?
15. Vhalzimar is the wizard who rules the tower at Sorrowmere.
16. Ka'zeth, the goblin you captured, starts laughing.
17. I ask Ka'zeth why he's laughing.
18. Ka'zeth says, "Orrin will find you before the snow comes."
19. Orrin is the captain of the guard in Thornewick.
20. I cast Detect Magic on the amulet.
21. The amulet glows faintly. It once belonged to Ysolde.
22. Ysolde and Elowen were sisters, long ago.
23. Quillon, the halfling, wants to buy the amulet.
24. I'm not selling it to Quillon.
25. Kael, you hear footsteps on the roof.
26. Kael climbs up to take a look.
27. Make a Dexterity saving throw. The DC is fifteen.
28. That's a fourteen. Kael slips and falls.
29. You take two d6 plus three bludgeoning damage.
30. Nyxara the priestess runs over to help.
31. Nyxara casts Cure Wounds on Kael.
32. Gorrak and his orcs block the north road.
33. Gorrak demands a toll of fifty gold pieces.
34. Tamsin, you recognize one of the orcs.
35. Tamsin uses Bardic Inspiration on Belleros.
36. Belleros casts Fireball at the orcs.
37. Roll initiative, everyone.
38. Bell, you're up first.
39. Bell and Cerric flank the orc on the left.
40. Zephyrine, the storm sorcerer, watches from the hill.
41. Zephyrine and Lirael are both members of the Ashen Crown.
42. The Ashen Crown worships Varrow, the god of winter.
43. A prayer to Varrow echoes across the valley.
44. Saelith whispers that Ilvaris has been lying to you.
45. Ilvaris and Oskar Vane meet in secret at Sorrowmere.
46. Oskar Vane leaves a letter for Elowen in Thornewick.
47. I want to go back to Brynwater and find Hrothgar.
48. Let's take a short rest before we go on.

**Tricky everyday lines** (no names: nothing here should turn into a name)

49. He has a serrated blade, so he'll saw through the rope.
50. Ring the bell, or else they'll hear us.
51. My cousin Eric is visiting next week.
52. Sarah from work says hi to everyone.
53. Put the quill on the desk, please.
54. That bridge is old and the boards are rotten.
55. Grab an arrow from the barrow by the door.
56. The kale salad is in the fridge if anyone's hungry.
57. The sky turned orange as the sun went down.

**Off-topic talk**

58. Is anyone else free next Thursday?
59. Hang on, my dog is barking at the mail carrier.
60. Did anybody watch the game last night?
61. I have to leave at ten tonight, I've got work early.

**Everyday lines**

62. The weather has been really cold this week.
63. Can you pass me the dice and the snacks?
64. I think we should go left at the next crossroads.
65. Thanks everyone, that was a great session.

## Scoring key

- **Name lines (1–48):** each name said is one point. Lines 38–39 test the nickname
  **Bell**, which must stay "Bell" (not be expanded to Belleros).
- **Tricky lines (49–57):** each name written where none was said is a **false name**.
  "Sarah" and "Eric" in lines 51–52 are real words here and must stay.
- **Off-topic (58–61)** and **everyday lines (62–65):** word error rate, and any false
  name.
