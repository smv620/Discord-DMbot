# DMbot test script: the bake-off story

A short story for the session twin's name test (#367). You read every **[DM]** line out loud,
alone. It takes about three minutes. It uses every name from
[stt-bakeoff.md](stt-bakeoff.md), each at least twice.

**How to read**
- Record it on your phone or computer (not through DMbot), in a quiet room. Save it as
  `bakeoff-story.m4a`.
- Don't read the words in **[ ]** or **(( ))** out loud. They tell you what to do.
- Read at a normal pace, as if you were running a game. Say the names the way the guide below
  says, without over-pronouncing them.
- **(( dramatic pause ))**: stop and stay quiet while you count slowly to three, then go on.
- **(( whispering ))**: whisper the sentence in *italics*, then use your normal voice again.
- Stumbled? Stop, then say the whole sentence again.

**[Don't read this out loud.]** Say the names like this (capitals = stressed part): Cerric
(SER-ik), Belleros (bel-LAIR-os), Hrothgar (ROTH-gar), Vhalzimar (VAL-zih-mar), Ka'zeth
(kah-ZETH), Mirelle (mih-REL), Orrin (OR-in), Ysolde (ih-SOLD), Quillon (KWIL-on), Dravenmoor
(DRAY-ven-moor), Brynwater (BRIN-water), Kael (KAYL), Nyxara (nix-AR-ah), Thornewick
(THORN-wick), Ilvaris (il-VAR-is), Sorrowmere (SOR-oh-meer), Gorrak (GOR-ak), Elowen
(EL-oh-wen), Varrow (VAIR-oh), Zephyrine (ZEF-ih-reen), Tamsin (TAM-zin), Lirael
(LEER-ee-el), Saelith (SAY-lith), Oskar Vane (OS-kar VAYN).

## Part 1: the road north

**[DM]:** Cerric wakes before dawn in the lake town of Brynwater. The water is grey and
still. Belleros, his oldest friend, waits at the gate. Everyone calls him Bell. Bell has
never minded. Hrothgar the blacksmith hands them two mended shields. "Bring them back to Brynwater
in one piece," says Hrothgar.

**[DM]:** A letter comes from Mirelle, an elf who rarely writes. Mirelle warns them that the
wizard Vhalzimar is awake again. Vhalzimar rules a black tower at Sorrowmere, far to the
north. (( dramatic pause )) Nobody who walks to Sorrowmere comes back the same.

**[DM]:** On the road they meet Ka'zeth, a goblin with a crooked grin. Ka'zeth swears he knows
a short path through the marsh of Dravenmoor. Cerric does not trust him. The path through
Dravenmoor is cold and narrow, but it is quick.

**[DM]:** At noon a captain named Orrin stops them on the bridge. Orrin keeps the peace in
the town of Thornewick. "Thornewick is closed to strangers," he says. An old healer speaks up
for them, and her name is Ysolde. Ysolde has known Orrin since he was a boy, so they may stay.  
(( whispering )) *She gives them bread and a warm blanket for the night.*

## Part 2: the inn and the hill

**[DM]:** In the inn they find a quiet ranger. His name is Kael. Kael carries a long bow and
says very little. A cheerful halfling buys everyone a drink, and his name is Quillon.
Quillon talks too much, and nobody at the table trusts him. Nyxara the priestess sits by the fire. When Kael falls on the
stairs, Nyxara casts Cure Wounds and the cut closes at once.

**[DM]:** In the morning, Gorrak and his orcs block the road. Gorrak wants a toll that no one
can pay. The bard Tamsin steps forward and sings. Tamsin gives Bell Bardic Inspiration, and
Bell laughs out loud. Then Belleros casts Fireball. (( dramatic pause )) The orcs run, and
the smoke from the Fireball hangs over the road for an hour.

**[DM]:** From the hill, a sorcerer named Zephyrine watches them pass. She and her sister
Lirael serve the Ashen Crown. The Ashen Crown prays to Varrow. Varrow is the god of winter,
and a cold song drifts across the valley. Lirael says nothing at all. Zephyrine only smiles.

**[DM]:** That night, a stranger named Saelith finds them in the dark. Saelith whispers that
the scholar Ilvaris has lied to them. Ilvaris meets Oskar Vane in secret. Vane is a merchant
with a quiet voice and a heavy purse. Oskar Vane leaves a letter for a woman named Elowen.
Elowen reads it once and burns it. Before the sun comes up, Cerric casts Detect Magic on the
ashes. Detect Magic shows one word in the ash. Tamsin leans close and reads it aloud:
"Sorrowmere." (( dramatic pause )) Bardic Inspiration keeps their spirits up. Cure Wounds heals
the last of their burns, and they ride north.
