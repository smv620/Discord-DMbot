# DMbot test script: the bake-off story

A short story to test how well DMbot hears unusual names (#367). You read every **[DM]** line
out loud, alone. It takes about three minutes. It uses every name from
[stt-bakeoff.md](stt-bakeoff.md), each at least twice.

**How to read**
- Record it on your phone or computer, not through DMbot. Use a quiet room: no music or TV.
  Keep the phone the same distance from your mouth the whole time.
- Save it as `bakeoff-story`. Any audio type is fine (.m4a, .mp3 or .wav).
- When you're done, send the file to the web session (as you did with the bake-off
  recording), so it can be added next to this script.
- Don't read the words in **[ ]** or **(( ))** out loud. They tell you what to do.
- Read at a normal pace, as if you were running a game. Say the names as shown below,
  without over-pronouncing them.
- **Between two [DM] lines**: stop and count to two in your head, then go on.
- **(( dramatic pause ))**: stop and stay quiet while you count slowly to three, then go on.
- **(( whispering ))**: whisper the sentence in *italics*, then use your normal voice again.
- Stumbled? Say the sentence again and carry on. Afterwards, write down which sentences you
  said twice.

## Part 1: the road north

**[DM]:** Cerric wakes before dawn in the lake town of Brynwater. The water is grey and
still. Belleros, his oldest friend, waits at the gate.

**[DM]:** Everyone calls him Bell. Bell has never minded. Hrothgar the blacksmith hands them
two mended shields.

**[DM]:** "Bring them back to Brynwater in one piece," says Hrothgar.

**[DM]:** A letter comes from Mirelle, an elf who rarely writes. Mirelle warns them that the
wizard Vhalzimar is awake again.

**[DM]:** Vhalzimar rules a black tower at Sorrowmere, far to the north. (( dramatic pause ))
Nobody who walks to Sorrowmere comes back the same.

**[DM]:** On the road they meet Ka'zeth, a goblin with a crooked grin. Ka'zeth swears he knows
a short path through the marsh of Dravenmoor.

**[DM]:** Cerric does not trust him. The path through Dravenmoor is cold and narrow, but it is
quick.

**[DM]:** At noon a captain named Orrin stops them on the bridge. Orrin keeps the peace in the
town of Thornewick.

**[DM]:** "Thornewick is closed to strangers," he says. An old healer speaks up for them, and
her name is Ysolde.

**[DM]:** Ysolde has known Orrin since he was a boy, so they may stay.  
(( whispering )) *She gives them bread and a warm blanket for the night.*

## Part 2: the inn and the hill

**[DM]:** In the inn they find a quiet ranger. His name is Kael. Kael carries a long bow and
says very little.

**[DM]:** A cheerful halfling buys everyone a drink, and his name is Quillon. Quillon talks
too much, and nobody at the table trusts him.

**[DM]:** Nyxara the priestess sits by the fire. When Kael falls on the stairs, Nyxara casts
Cure Wounds and the cut closes at once.

**[DM]:** In the morning, Gorrak and his orcs block the road. Gorrak wants a toll that no one
can pay.

**[DM]:** The bard Tamsin steps forward and sings. Tamsin gives Bardic Inspiration to his
friend Bell. Then Belleros casts Fireball.

**[DM]:** The orcs run, and the smoke from the Fireball hangs over the road for an hour.

**[DM]:** From the hill, a sorcerer named Zephyrine watches them pass. She and her sister
Lirael serve the Ashen Crown.

**[DM]:** The Ashen Crown prays to Varrow. Varrow is the god of winter, and a cold song drifts
across the valley.

**[DM]:** Lirael says nothing at all. Zephyrine only smiles.

**[DM]:** That night, a stranger named Saelith finds them in the dark. Saelith whispers that
the scholar Ilvaris has lied to them.

**[DM]:** Ilvaris meets Oskar Vane in secret. Vane is a merchant with a quiet voice and a heavy
purse.

**[DM]:** Oskar Vane leaves a letter for a woman named Elowen. Elowen reads it once and burns
it.

**[DM]:** Before the sun comes up, Cerric casts Detect Magic on the ashes. Detect Magic shows
one word in the ash.

**[DM]:** Tamsin leans close and reads it aloud: "Sorrowmere." (( dramatic pause )) Bardic
Inspiration keeps their spirits up.

**[DM]:** Cure Wounds heals the last of their burns, and they ride north.
