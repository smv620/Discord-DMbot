# DMbot test script: two voices, recorded alone

The [DM and Player](dm-and-player.md) script, cut so that **one person can record both
voices**, one after the other. DMbot's test copy then plays them back as two people at a
table (#534). Each recording takes about two minutes.

**How to record**
- Record on your phone or computer, not through DMbot. Use a quiet room. Use the same room,
  the same phone and the same distance from your mouth both times. You can use a different
  voice for the player.
- Make **two recordings**:
  1. **The DM recording**: read only the **[DM]** lines. When you stop, rename the file
     **two-voices-dm** (keep its ending, such as .m4a).
  2. **The Player recording**: read only the **[Player]** lines. Rename it
     **two-voices-player**.
- Each recording goes through **every cue from 1 to 8**, in order. Before each cue, look
  at the name in brackets: if it's yours, read the line; if it isn't, **stay quiet for
  about ten seconds**. Count slowly in your head, "one-and, two-and…", up to ten. Don't
  rush it: that quiet is how DMbot tells one of your turns from the next, so never skip it.
- **Starting:** press record, then begin at Cue 1. The Player recording starts with ten
  seconds of quiet, because Cue 1 is the DM's.
- **Finishing:** after your last line, stay quiet for two seconds, then stop. (The DM's last
  line is Cue 7.)
- Any audio type is fine (.m4a, .mp3 or .wav). Send both files to the web session, the same
  way you sent the bake-off recording. Two files, two names: if both are called "New
  Recording", nobody can tell which voice is which.

**While reading**
- Don't read the words in **[ ]**, **(( ))** or the cue headings out loud.
- Read at a normal pace, as if you were playing.
- **(( dramatic pause ))**: stop talking for about **three seconds** (count "one-and,
  two-and, three-and"), then go on. No muting here: just stop talking. Don't count to ten
  here, or it will sound like the other person's turn.
- **(( whispering ))**: whisper the sentence in *italics*, then use your normal voice again.
- Stumbled? Say the sentence again **straight away**. If you stopped for long or lost your
  place, stop the recording and start that file again from Cue 1.

## Part 1: everyday words

### Cue 1 (DM)

**[DM]:** Your story starts in a small town at the edge of the woods.

### Cue 2 (Player)

**[Player]:** I knock on the front door three times.

### Cue 3 (DM)

**[DM]:** An old man opens the door. (( dramatic pause )) "Come inside," he says. "It's cold."

### Cue 4 (Player)

**[Player]:** Thank you.  
(( whispering )) *We need hot food and a warm bed.*

## Part 2: D&D words

**[Don't read this out loud.]** Say the names like this: Bryn Shander (BRIN SHAN-der),
Targos (TAR-gohs), Auril (AW-ril), Caer-Dineval (KAIR DIN-eh-vahl).

### Cue 5 (DM)

**[DM]:** Welcome to Bryn Shander, the biggest of the Ten-Towns. The road goes north to
Targos and east to Easthaven.

### Cue 6 (Player)

**[Player]:** I cast Detect Magic on the frozen chest.

### Cue 7 (DM)

**[DM]:** Make a Dexterity saving throw. (( dramatic pause )) A frost giant walks out of the
blizzard. Auril the Frostmaiden is watching.

### Cue 8 (Player)

**[Player]:** I draw my longsword and shout, "For Lonelywood and Caer-Dineval!"
