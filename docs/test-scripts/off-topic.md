# DMbot test script: off-topic chat, recorded alone

For the off-topic filter (#52): a short scene, a stretch of chat that has nothing to do with
the game, then the scene again. The session twin plays it back as one person at a table, so
the cleaned transcript can show the off-topic chat replaced by one marker, with no table
needed. About two minutes.

**How to record**
- Record on your phone or computer, not through DMbot, in a quiet room.
- Read each line, then stay quiet for about two seconds before the next. At **(( pause ))**,
  stay quiet for about five seconds.
- Any audio type is fine. Send the file to the Supervisor session; it goes next to this
  script as `off-topic.m4a`.

**While reading**
- Don't read the line numbers, or the words in **[ ]** or **(( ))**, out loud.
- Read the chat part as you'd really chat: it's fine to sound bored or laugh.
- Read the lines as written; don't swap in your own real plans or names (the recording is
  public).

---

**[Game]**

1. The road climbs into the hills, and the wind carries snow off the peaks.
2. I draw my sword and keep watch while the others make camp.
3. Roll a perception check for me, please.
4. Fourteen. Do I see anything out there?

(( pause ))

**[Chat: nothing to do with the game]**

5. Oh, before I forget, my sister's wedding is the Saturday after next.
6. So I can't come that week, I'll be driving down Friday night.
7. Did anyone see the match last night? That last goal was unbelievable.
8. I still can't believe they sold their best striker in the summer.
9. Anyway, who wants to order food? I'm thinking Thai this time.
10. Last time the noodles took over an hour, so let's order early.

(( pause ))

**[Game again]**

11. Back to it. Something moves behind the rocks, low and fast.
12. I nock an arrow and call out, who goes there?
13. A wolf steps into the firelight, then another, and another.

---

**What the twin should show**
- **As heard:** all 13 lines.
- **Cleaned:** lines 1 to 4 and 11 to 13 as they were. Lines 5 to 10 become one line, the
  marker with how long the chat was spoken (pauses don't count), about 25 to 35 seconds,
  such as `[0:00:31] (Reader) [28s of off-topic chat skipped]`. Line 3 ("Roll a perception
  check") is table talk: it stays. Line 11 ("Back to it. Something moves…") starts as chat
  but is the game: it must stay.
- **When unsure, keep it:** if a chat line is kept, that's acceptable; a game line hidden is
  a failure.
- **Cost:** the session's log line "Off-topic filter: N calls, … tokens" should show one or
  two calls for the chat part. Lines 2, 3 and 12 ("draw my sword", "perception check",
  "nock an arrow") may never be sent: plainly game talk isn't asked about.
