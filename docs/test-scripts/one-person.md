# DMbot test script: one person

Read this out loud in DMbot's voice channel. It takes under a minute.

- Read at a normal speaking pace, as if you were playing.
- **(( dramatic pause ))**: stop talking and stay silent for about **3 seconds**, then go on.
- **(( whispering ))**: whisper only that sentence, then go back to your normal voice.

## Part 1: everyday words

**[DM]:** Good evening, everyone. Tonight your story starts in a small town at the edge of
the woods. (( dramatic pause )) An old man opens his door and says, come inside, it is
getting cold. We need hot food, a warm bed, and a map of the roads.

## Part 2: D&D words

**[DM]:** Welcome to Bryn Shander, the biggest of the Ten-Towns. The road goes north to
Targos and east to Easthaven. (( whispering )) I cast Detect Magic on the frozen chest.
Make a Dexterity saving throw. (( dramatic pause )) A frost giant walks out of the
blizzard. Auril the Frostmaiden is watching, from Lonelywood to Caer-Dineval.
